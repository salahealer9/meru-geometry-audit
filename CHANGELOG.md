## [0.8.0] — 2026-08-04

### Added

- Preregistered FIRST HAND endpoint-parallelism audit for the source-supported
  reciprocal spiral \(r\theta=1\) under the AOG-PROSE and AOG-DIAGRAM
  truncation branches.
- Intrinsic spherical tangent comparison using parallel transport and
  independent ambient directed/unoriented endpoint comparisons.
- Fixed comparator study covering reciprocal, endpoint-matched Archimedean,
  generic logarithmic, and Golden-Mean logarithmic spirals.
- Preregistered truncation-parity audit at 1, 1.5, 2, and 2.5 turns.
- Source-constrained Variant-B dimpled-sphere carrier family with a frozen
  400-cell sweep over carrier width, elongation, spiral family, and scale.
- Read-only Variant-B throat-width trend analysis over the frozen sweep.
- Bounded FIRST HAND construction source-recovery protocol with Tier A
  primary-source, Tier B historical/explanatory, and Tier C legal/registration
  provenance passes.
- Source-recovery ledger, reference tracker, archived A01-A10 re-audit,
  tier matrices, tier closeouts, and final construction-source closeout.

### Verified

- No preregistered Variant-A reciprocal branch satisfies the directed endpoint
  parallelism criterion under intrinsic spherical comparison.
- The same Variant-A reciprocal construction also fails directed ambient and
  unoriented line comparison at the registered scales.
- Comparator ranking is truncation-parity dependent: integer and odd-half-turn
  truncations reverse the relative behavior of reciprocal and logarithmic
  families.
- No reciprocal or logarithmic cell in the frozen 400-cell Variant-B sweep
  reaches exact directed or unoriented endpoint parallelism.
- For matched Variant-B cells, directed semantics favors the reciprocal family
  in all 350 reciprocal-vs-log comparisons, while unoriented line semantics
  favors the logarithmic family in all 350 comparisons.
- The registered Variant-B width trends are predominantly non-monotone, so the
  source statement that wider holes move the twist deeper does not define a
  universal monotonic endpoint-alignment law.
- The registered published Meru/legal corpus contains substantial reciprocal,
  spherical, topological, tetrahedral, and projection scaffolding, but no
  reproducible deterministic continuous construction of the finished
  endpoint-parallel FIRST HAND meeting the preregistered recovery criterion.

`NO_REPRODUCIBLE_PUBLISHED_FIRST_HAND_CONSTRUCTION_RECOVERED`

### Clarified

- The published reciprocal law is a recovered seed/scaffold ingredient and is
  not silently equated with the finished physical sculpture.
- The only inspected construction route that explicitly guarantees endpoint
  parallelism does so by manual deformation; this does not prove that the
  claimed unrecovered algebraic constructions never existed.
- Historical turn-count language changed from an early 3.5-turn full-vortex
  convention to a later 3-turn convention; later 1.5-turn FIRST HAND statements
  must be compared using the later convention.
- Meru's Tetrahelix Accounting places 3 turns, 99 tetrahedra, and six
  1.5-turn HANDS in the same 3,10 structure, ruling out a naive interpretation
  of the six HANDS as six disjoint 49-tetrahedron partitions.
- Copyright protection of the sculpture does not imply publication of an
  enabling mathematical construction; no FIRST HAND patent disclosure was
  located in the bounded registered patent search.
- Optional archival Tier-D material may be examined later without reopening
  the bounded Tiers A-C v0.8.0 result.

## [0.7.0] — 2026-07-30

### Added

- Official Meru digital-source catalogue with frozen source identities,
  sizes and SHA-256 hashes.
- Native `10_3.wrl` centreline recovery from the 300 consecutive
  20-vertex tube sections.
- Deterministic toroidal winding audit.
- Exhaustive numerical surface-embedding certificate for the complete
  12,000-triangle tube.
- Independent three-strand braid reconstruction and Alexander
  polynomial audit.
- A10_P03–`tumble.gif` schematic correspondence record.
- Regression tests covering source identity, topology, embedding,
  braid structure, knot invariant and interpretation boundaries.

### Verified

- 6,000 vertices, 12,000 triangular faces and 18,000 mesh edges.
- Closed, connected and consistently oriented genus-one mesh.
- Signed toroidal winding `(3, -10)` and unsigned pair `{3, 10}`.
- All 71,994,000 distinct triangular-face pairs accounted for.
- Zero vertex-disjoint face intersections.
- Zero excess intersections among shared-edge and shared-vertex face
  pairs.
- Recovered braid
  `(\sigma_2^-1 sigma_1^-1)^10`.
- Twenty crossings, writhe `-20` and one-component braid closure.
- Alexander polynomial equal to the \(T(3,10)\) polynomial, with degree
  18 and `|Delta(-1)| = 3`.
- Full regression suite: 227 tests passing.

### Clarified

- A10_P03 is a simplified schematic, not a complete planar knot
  diagram.
- `tumble.gif`, `10_3.wrl` and A10_P03 are related Meru
  representations but are not metrically identical geometries.
- `1_3-3_1B.wrl` belongs to the separate 3-around-1 / 1-around-3
  construction and is excluded from the 3,10 correspondence audit.
- Chirality statements are convention-relative.
- Numerical certificates are not described as formal exact-arithmetic
  proofs.
