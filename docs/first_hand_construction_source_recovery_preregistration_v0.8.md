# First Hand Construction Source-Recovery Preregistration

**Checkpoint:** `first_hand_construction_source_recovery_preregistration_v0.8`  
**Status:** PREREGISTERED SOURCE-RECOVERY PROTOCOL — NO NEW CONSTRUCTION SEARCH AFTER FREEZE UNTIL COMMIT  
**Phase:** historical reconstruction after Variant-A and Variant-B analytic checkpoints  
**Primary target:** recover a source-specified reproducible FIRST HAND construction  
**Secondary targets:** recover the claimed optimum-hole definition; resolve turn-count terminology; recover relationships among multiple claimed constructions  
**Numerical fitting:** prohibited  
**Adaptive corpus expansion:** prohibited within this checkpoint

## 1. Purpose

The completed Variant-A and Variant-B checkpoints tested explicit audit reconstructions of Stan Tenen / Meru Foundation FIRST HAND geometry.

Those tests did not recover the claimed endpoint-parallel geometry for the registered reciprocal realizations.

The source, however, states that FIRST HAND forms can be produced by:

- three sets of specially arranged algebraic functions;
- at least one geometric construction;
- or, for visual purposes, manual bending until the outer end is parallel to the inner tip.

The source also states that all of the algebraic/geometric FIRST HAND constructions appear identical and self-embed.

The next scientific question is therefore historical and reconstructive:

> **Does the finite published Meru corpus contain enough information to reproduce any claimed FIRST HAND construction deterministically, without choosing continuous free parameters from audit results?**

This checkpoint preregisters:

1. the source corpus;
2. the search questions;
3. the search vocabulary;
4. the recovery criterion;
5. the equivalence requirement if multiple constructions are found;
6. the stopping rule;
7. explicit positive null states.

The aim is to prevent an open-ended search in which absence can never be concluded.

## 2. Known evidence before this preregistration

The following facts were already known before this source-recovery protocol was frozen and are therefore **prior evidence**, not discoveries produced by the registered recovery search.

### K01 — Three algebraic sets and one geometric construction are claimed

The Golden Mean / FIRST HAND source states that Meru knows of:

- three sets of specially arranged algebraic functions;
- at least one geometric construction;

which produce FIRST HAND vortex sculptures.

It further states that all appear identical and self-embed because their outer ends and inner tips are parallel.

### K02 — Manual bending is explicitly permitted as a visual construction route

The same source states that, for visual and aesthetic purposes, one could simply bend the vortex until its outer end becomes parallel to its inner tip.

Therefore the physical sculpture need not necessarily instantiate one uniquely recoverable closed-form analytic construction.

### K03 — An "optimum" Dimpled-Sphere hole is explicitly invoked but not defined on the known page

The source states that the displayed Golden Mean vortices were deliberately projected onto Dimpled-Sphere tori with holes that are:

> smaller and narrower than optimum.

The stated reasons are to make the endpoint-angle distinction easier to see and to preserve ordinary-sphere-like exterior geometry over most of the outside.

The source further states that:

- wider holes make the spiral twist deeper into the dimple;
- narrower holes keep the twist nearer the top;
- elongation changes the outer end somewhat.

No metric definition of the claimed optimum hole had been recovered before this preregistration.

### K04 — Published comparator images are not neutral calibration images of the claimed optimum carrier

Because the source explicitly says the displayed Golden Mean examples use non-optimum hole geometry for explanatory visibility, those figures may not be treated as metric calibration of the claimed optimum FIRST HAND carrier.

This does not imply deceptive manipulation.

It is simply a source-declared pedagogical choice.

### K05 — Turn-count terminology changed historically

Meru's introduction to *The Light in the Meeting Tent* explicitly warns that its historical turn counts used an older convention.

It gives the example that the early "3 1/2-turn" vortex is now called a "3-turn" vortex and says:

- the vortex itself is the same;
- the counting convention changed for mathematical consistency.

Therefore previously logged apparent conflicts involving "3-turn", "3 1/2-turn", and "1 1/2-turn" terminology must not be treated automatically as geometric contradictions.

### K06 — Arm-of-God turn-count issue is downgraded pending terminology resolution

The existing audit item involving:

- the "3-Turn Spiral Vortex Edge" wording for the 7-color torus;
- and the explicit reciprocal-spiral construction span of \(1.5\) turns / \(3\pi\);

is provisionally classified:

`PENDING_TURN_COUNT_TERMINOLOGY_RESOLUTION`

rather than:

`CONFIRMED_SOURCE_CONTRADICTION`.

This source-recovery checkpoint must determine whether the labels refer to different counting conventions, different geometric objects, or a genuine unresolved inconsistency.

### K07 — The relevant Meru geometry is documented through copyright

The 1998 federal court decision in *Tenen v. Winter* identifies Tenen's three-dimensional sculpture and states that *The Light in the Meeting Tent* was registered with the U.S. Copyright Office.

Copies of later findings/order materials report sculpture registrations including *The Ideal Hand (Left & Right)* and *Hand, Finger Width (Left & Right)*.

Exact sculpture-registration metadata should be treated as an archival/legal-source item to verify through the best available authoritative record.

### K08 — No FIRST HAND patent disclosure has been located in searches performed before this preregistration

A previously suggested Dutch patent citation, `NL7015469A`, is not a Tenen invention and must not be used as evidence about Tenen's patent history.

An unrelated U.S. patent, `US3909666A`, *Ballast circuit for gaseous discharge lamps*, identifies Stanley N. Tenen as inventor.

Thus the correct prior statement is:

> **Stanley N. Tenen held at least one unrelated patent, but no patent disclosure for the FIRST HAND geometry has been located in the searches performed to date.**

This is not a universal patent-office clearance statement.

### K09 — Copyright posture does not imply an enabling geometric disclosure

Copyright protection of a sculptural work does not, by itself, require publication of a deterministic mathematical construction.

Therefore absence of a public construction would not be surprising merely because the sculpture was registered.

This is contextual inference, not proof that a construction was intentionally withheld.

This checkpoint is limited to determining whether a reproducible construction is present in a finite defined corpus.

It is not:

- a new numerical carrier sweep;
- a parameter optimization;
- a reverse-engineering fit to photographs;
- a copyright-law opinion;
- a patentability analysis;
- a search for every mention of Meru anywhere on the internet.

## 4. Primary recovery questions

The search must answer the following named questions.

### RQ1 — Algebraic construction recovery

Can any one of the claimed three algebraic function sets be recovered with enough explicit information to generate a unique FIRST HAND curve?

### RQ2 — Algebraic-set multiplicity

Can two or all three claimed algebraic function sets be recovered?

If so, does the source explicitly claim that they generate the same geometry?

### RQ3 — Geometric construction recovery

Can the claimed geometric construction be recovered as a deterministic finite sequence of geometric operations?

### RQ4 — Optimum-hole definition

Does any registered source define quantitatively what Meru means by the "optimum" Dimpled-Sphere hole?

Relevant quantities include, but are not limited to:

- throat radius;
- hole width;
- hole depth;
- carrier elongation;
- relation to spiral pitch;
- relation to endpoint parallelism;
- relation to 3,10 torus-knot geometry.

### RQ5 — Spiral-to-carrier mapping

Does any registered source uniquely specify how the planar reciprocal / hyperbolic spiral is mapped, projected, bent, or otherwise transformed into the three-dimensional FIRST HAND vortex?

### RQ6 — Turn-count terminology

Can the historical labels:

- 3 1/2-turn;
- 3-turn;
- 1 1/2-turn;
- three loops;
- half of the 3-turn / 7-color-map structure;

be reconciled explicitly from source terminology?

### RQ7 — Outer-turn bending

Does any registered source specify the "outer turns bent back" operation mathematically or geometrically rather than descriptively?

### RQ8 — Hand-adjusted versus generated form

Does the source distinguish reproducibly between:

- a mathematically generated FIRST HAND form;
- a hand-bent aesthetically adjusted form;
- and the physical sculptures used in published demonstrations?

### RQ9 — Copyright-deposit relevance

Do accessible legal or copyright records identify deposited materials that plausibly contain more than photographs / identifying material and might contain construction instructions?

This is an archival metadata question only.

The actual archival deposit need not be obtained for the published-corpus null state to be valid.

## 5. Recovery criterion

A claimed construction counts as:

`REPRODUCIBLE_CONSTRUCTION_RECOVERED`

only if the source determines the resulting curve or finite geometric object without continuous parameters being chosen by the audit from output behavior.

At least one of the following forms must be satisfied.

### 5.1 Explicit analytic form

The source provides equations such as

\[
x(t),\qquad y(t),\qquad z(t),
\]

or an equivalent parametric / implicit representation, with all required constants specified or derived from source-fixed quantities.

### 5.2 Explicit composition of functions

The source gives a finite sequence of functions or transforms sufficient to generate the three-dimensional curve uniquely.

### 5.3 Deterministic geometric construction

The source specifies a finite construction in which every operation is determined, for example:

- construct specified points / circles / spheres / surfaces;
- take specified intersections;
- rotate by specified angles;
- project by a specified projection;
- choose a uniquely specified branch;
- trim at source-fixed endpoints.

### 5.4 Finite discrete ambiguity

A construction may still count as recovered if the source leaves only a finite, explicitly enumerable discrete ambiguity.

Every discrete branch must be retained.

The audit may not select one branch after seeing which best matches a sculpture.

---

## 6. What does not count as recovery

The following are classified:

`PARTIAL_UNDERDETERMINED_CONSTRUCTION`

rather than reproducible recovery:

- "bend until the ends are parallel";
- "use the optimum hole" without defining optimum;
- a picture with no deterministic construction;
- a reciprocal spiral plus an unspecified projection;
- a torus plus an unspecified deformation;
- equations containing an unfixed continuous constant;
- instructions requiring an aesthetic choice;
- a construction requiring audit-fitted dimple width, elongation, rotation, scale, or truncation;
- a hand-drawn line from which the audit must infer the governing formula;
- prose stating that several constructions exist without giving them.

A numerical fit to the physical sculpture is explicitly prohibited as a substitute for source recovery.

## 7. Mutual-agreement requirement if multiple constructions are recovered

The source states that the multiple FIRST HAND constructions appear identical.

If two or more reproducible constructions are recovered, this source claim becomes separately testable.

The recovery phase itself must record:

- each construction independently;
- its source location;
- its complete equations / operations;
- its remaining discrete ambiguities;
- the source's claimed relationship among them.

No recovered construction may be declared the canonical one merely because it appears first or matches a preferred outcome.

After source recovery is frozen, a separate preregistration must test mutual agreement.

The required future state names are reserved now:

`RECOVERED_CONSTRUCTIONS_MUTUALLY_EQUIVALENT`

`RECOVERED_CONSTRUCTIONS_NOT_MUTUALLY_EQUIVALENT`

`RECOVERED_CONSTRUCTION_EQUIVALENCE_INDETERMINATE`

No equivalence test may be run before its own implementation boundary is frozen.

## 20. Copyright / deposit interpretation rule

Copyright registration may establish:

- authorship/claimant metadata;
- approximate chronology;
- existence of a protected sculptural work;
- potentially the nature of deposited identifying material.

Copyright registration does not, by itself, establish:

- that a mathematical construction was deposited;
- that the construction was publicly disclosed;
- that equations exist;
- that unpublished construction material is accessible.

Therefore the following inference is allowed:

> The relevant Meru works were protected through copyright, so no patent-style enabling disclosure can be assumed.

The following inference is prohibited without direct evidence:

> Tenen deliberately concealed the equations through copyright.

---

## 21. Stopping rule

The registered published-corpus search stops when all of the following are true:

1. every Tier-A item has been inspected and logged;
2. every Tier-B item admitted under the fixed rules has been inspected and logged;
3. every Tier-C item practically accessible through the registered legal/catalog sources has been inspected and logged;
4. every registered search term has been applied to the Tier-A corpus;
5. all direct-link expansions admitted under Section 13 have been inspected;
6. all construction references found within the registered corpus either:
   - resolve to an inspected registered source;
   - resolve to an unavailable/non-public item and are logged as such;
   - or are bibliographically insufficient to retrieve.

At that point the published-corpus state must be assigned.

The search does not continue merely because a reproducible construction has not been found.

---

## 22. Tier-D stopping rule

Tier D is closed independently when any one of the following holds:

1. the relevant deposit is obtained and classified;
2. authoritative metadata states that public inspection/copying is unavailable under the applicable access route;
3. the archive requires a process outside the practical scope of v0.8 and this is documented;
4. the registered archival request/search path has been exhausted without retrieval.

Tier-D non-retrieval does not block the Tiers A-C null state.

## 25. Expected-yield discipline

The expected-yield labels are methodological, not probabilistic estimates.

They exist to prevent the null state from being distorted by a low-yield inaccessible archive.

### Tier A

Expected yield: `HIGH`

Reason: direct construction claims and earliest working documents.

### Tier B

Expected yield: `MODERATE_TO_LOW`

Reason: terminology and historical references may point to missing primary material but are less likely to contain complete equations.

### Tier C

Expected yield: `LOW_FOR_GEOMETRY_MODERATE_FOR_PROVENANCE`

Reason: legal and registration records may identify works/exhibits but are not technical disclosures by default.

### Tier D

Expected yield: `LOW_AND_ACCESS_DEPENDENT`

Reason: sculpture deposits may consist primarily of identifying visual material and may not be publicly inspectable.

A Tier-D null or access failure must not weaken a properly completed Tier-A-C published-corpus null.

## 26. What happens if a reproducible construction is recovered

No newly recovered construction may be executed immediately.

The required sequence is:

1. record the source and exact construction;
2. classify whether it is unique or discretely ambiguous;
3. finish the registered source-recovery corpus unless a safety/legal boundary prevents it;
4. freeze the source-recovery closeout;
5. if multiple constructions were recovered, preregister their mutual-agreement test;
6. preregister implementation of the recovered construction;
7. freeze implementation-only code;
8. execute only then.

No recovered parameter may be adjusted to match the existing sculpture or prior Variant-A/B results.

## 27. What happens on the likely null

If the registered Tiers A-C are exhausted and no reproducible construction is recovered, the checkpoint closes as:

`NO_REPRODUCIBLE_PUBLISHED_FIRST_HAND_CONSTRUCTION_RECOVERED`

The allowed conclusion is:

> **The registered published Meru/legal corpus does not provide a reproducible FIRST HAND construction meeting the audit's predeclared recovery criterion.**

If supported by the source record, the discussion may add:

> The surviving public record emphasizes copyrighted sculptural expression and descriptive construction claims, and does not supply a patent-style enabling disclosure of the FIRST HAND geometry.

It must not claim intentional withholding without direct evidence.

The null is a substantive historical result.
