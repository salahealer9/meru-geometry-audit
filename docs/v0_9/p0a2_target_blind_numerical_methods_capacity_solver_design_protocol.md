## 5. D3 — Effective viewing-capacity design and mathematical certification plan

For *orthographic ordered-centreline or compatible support* lanes with free planar SO(2), define the signed viewing map \(n=R^\mathsf{T}e_z\) and quotient \(SO(2)\backslash SO(3)\cong S^2\). Opposite directions are not universally identified under SO(2); under separately allowed planar O(2), identify \(n\sim -n\). The v0.5 group yields exactly G0 = `{±e_x, ±e_y, ±e_z}` (six signed directions) or three axes under O(2), conditional on object/observation semantics. Roll is **not a capacity dimension** under this quotient. Object-specific extra projection identities are not general-direction equivalences.

Require **at least two target-independent reproducible finite nested-capacity design candidates**, each described symbolically by exact coordinates or a deterministic generator, with canonical frame lifts, orientation precision, and proof obligations for no accidental duplicates and inclusion \(G_0\subsetneq G_1\subsetneq G_2\). Every proposed tier must retain G0 exactly. Include a reasoned review of this *illustrative, not yet selected* symmetric family:

- G0 = six coordinate-axis unit vectors;
- G1 may adjoin all eight cube-corner unit vectors \((\pm1,\pm1,\pm1)/\sqrt3\) → **14 distinct signed directions**;
- G2 may further adjoin the twelve edge-direction unit vectors obtained from permutations of \((\pm1,\pm1,0)/\sqrt2\) → **26 distinct signed directions**.

The 6/14/26 signed counts (3/7/13 axes under O(2)) are **combinatorial design examples**, **not historical Meru directions**, certified sphere-covering radii, selected final tiers, or a tested optimum. An alternative family must also be specified, not just an axial-roll augmentation. Do not tune tiers to target shapes. A finite direction count alone does not certify optimization over remaining planar similarity variables.

For each candidate design require: exact membership/set inclusion proof; quotient-distinct counts with reflection convention; minimum angular separation and angular covering radius *method and planned independent certificate* (values only if proved analytically, not guessed); ordering, antipodal pairing, tie handling, deterministic frame lift \(n\mapsto R_n\) and seam/canonicalization; actual nuisance/fitting budget; per-object equivalence reporting without post-target selection; future implementation and reference-oracle instructions. Compare capacity families on *a priori geometric coverage and computational tractability*, never on Hebrew score performance.

A continuous-domain proposal requires full parameter charts and a valid global numerical-bound strategy. A discretization statement \(E_\text{grid}-\inf_{n\in D}E(n)\le L\delta\) is **conditional** on a proved uniform Lipschitz/continuity bound for the complete objective, its optimizable nuisances and exclusion domains; otherwise label `COVERAGE_ERROR_UNCERTIFIED`. Occlusion, normalization degeneracy and thresholded rasters may invalidate that condition.

For nested fixed objective and identical nuisance domains, derive the **infimum** inequality \(\inf_{G_2}E\le\inf_{G_1}E\le\inf_{G_0}E\); only use `min` where attained. A lower optimized error follows from increased opportunity and demonstrates **no specificity** absent matched controls. Candidate and each future N0–N3 control must have the same metric, physical forward model, admissible domain, accuracy/failure policy and registered capacity definition.
