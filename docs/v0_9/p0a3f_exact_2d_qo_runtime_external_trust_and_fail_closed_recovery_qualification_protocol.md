## 0. Frozen authority, objective and reason for this narrower stage

P0A3D established a project-independently-QC-reviewed **abstract exact mathematical theorem** for two *independently supplied, genuinely ordered, open, finite 2D polygonal paths with rational-coordinate vertices*, normalized using **true planar arclength** and independent centroid/RMS and optimized over **proper rotations `SO(2)` only**. The human-signed theorem-result freeze is `59eb47f7500d57866e4935822b78a555d3bcb515`. That theorem is fixed; it is not a claim about Hebrew glyph observability or a historically recovered 3D FIRST HAND.

P0A3E independently improved parser/certificate/worker boundaries, but its human-signed incomplete preservation explicitly retains unqualified external code trust, environment-dependent worker memory behavior and incomplete hostile-input/crash guarantees. Passing 32 generic engineering DEV checks on `/usr/bin/python3` did **not** qualify a production executable. P0A3F shall **not** become a third omnibus implementation or replay earlier DEV merely to accumulate tests.

**Sole registered engineering question:** Can the already constructed exact-2D-QO candidate be made reproducibly executable and independently checkable **within a declared finite operational envelope**, with caller-owned expected identities, compatible interpreter preflight, supervised producer/checker processes, durable fail-closed recovery and qualified raw-input/certificate boundaries, while preserving the mathematical domain and making all unsupported host/resource conditions explicit?

The exact theorem states, for normalized paths `A,B` and `J(x,y)=(-y,x)`, that `alpha=∫B·A du`, `beta=∫B·J(A) du`, `s=+sqrt(alpha²+beta²)`, `qO=+sqrt(2−2s)` and, for `s>0`, the unique optimal proper rotation has matrix `[[alpha/s,−beta/s],[beta/s,alpha/s]]`. On exact `s=0`, every rotation ties, with identity the canonical witness. Irrational segment lengths from rational coordinates are admissible. This is a theorem over **all eligible finite rational inputs in unlimited exact arithmetic**, not a promise of finite-runtime completion for every such input.

**Unchanged exclusions:** no reversal, reflection, phase shift, arbitrary reparametrization, stroke/graph correspondence, trimming, free scale, physical optics, 3D camera, G0/A/B sets, QH, SEP/COV or continuous-sphere result. Separate historical source-rights, independent ordered-observation and fair N0–N3 control gates remain unresolved. The v0.8 `NO_REPRODUCIBLE_PUBLISHED_FIRST_HAND_CONSTRUCTION_RECOVERED` conclusion stands.
