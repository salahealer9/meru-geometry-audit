## 6. Continuous-geometry / exactness qualification barrier

V4's exact rational results were possible because all input objects were finite integer-coordinate polylines/polyhedra. The analytic candidates A and B involve trigonometric / transcendental functions and infinitely many points. Consequently, a V4 calculator applied to finitely many sample points **does not by itself prove** continuous analytic projection equivalence or inequivalence.

For each later numerical lane specify in advance:

1. **Mathematical object:** analytic parametric map, exact domain (open/closed endpoints and singular limits), regularity, pole/cusp conditions, traversal, and any self-intersections. Distinguish the whole projected trace from finite samples.
2. **Metric/equivalence:** translation policy, scale allowance (none unless separately justified), planar SO2/O2 branch, reversal, cyclic phase for closed curves, admissible correspondence, and equality versus approximately small distance. Fix *one mathematical estimand* before choosing the solver.
3. **Proof method:** either an exact symbolic identity/nonidentity certificate for every camera pair or a rigorous interval/subdivision scheme with stated coverage of the **entire parameter domain** and separation margins. A floating-point nearest-neighbour score, raster matcher or dense sample is not an equality proof.
4. **Certified enclosures:** rational/outward interval bounds for sin/cos, tan (where needed), exp/log and \(\pi\), plus defined guard handling around poles, almost-collapsed extents, retracing, tangent loss and end limits. Specify arithmetic precision and proved error budget before any evaluation. If an interval remains inconclusive, record `UNRESOLVED_PAIR` rather than break a tie by tolerance.
5. **Complexity parity:** numerical work, cells, shape parameters, camera directions, objective and admissible nuisance degrees must be ledgered for both candidate and null. Do not use one model's more favourable local optimum as a global certificate.
6. **Independent QC:** a second method, no import of the registered calculator, checks every camera, paired equivalence decision, failure/unknown entry, and rigorous analytic completeness assumptions. A lone computed class count is not sufficient.

**Important:** a finite dense sample and small Hausdorff distance can support numerical *proximity*, not logical identity or topological/silhouette equivalence. No later phase may label approximate classes exact by borrowing V4's PASS wording. For an unresolved continuous pair, its possible contribution to total class counts must be expressed as rigorous **lower/upper bounds** rather than arbitrarily assigned.

## 7. Candidate versus null study design without a specificity claim

A later registered V5B implementation can choose one modest finite branch with fully specified model curves, comparators and the same camera grid. Before first execution it must freeze:

- model and null instance IDs, exact or interval-representable equations and domains, metric maps, endpoint limits and scales;
- signed camera tier, SO2/O2 branches, 3D axis/pose choices, whether reversal/cyclic start is permitted, common centring and any scale normalizer;
- geometric observable, equivalence predicate, expected null-invariant diagnostic identities and refusal rules;
- finite matrix size, stopping rule, precision, memory/time budgets, handling of undecidable pairs and allowed rerun/amendment process;
- per-view curve and degeneracy records, pairwise equivalence *three-way* matrices (`EQUAL` / `UNEQUAL` / `UNRESOLVED`), class-count bounds, and numerator/denominator bookkeeping where exact predicates apply;
- a claim register separating `EXACT_CERTIFIED`, `INTERVAL_CERTIFIED`, `APPROXIMATE_ONLY`, `DEFERRED` and `FAILED`.

**Default staging recommendation (not permission to execute now):** begin with a two-scale **Variant-A DIAGRAM** branch, whose endpoint-matched radial-law controls already exist, and independently prove a continuous equality method on a small G0 camera set; then add A/B G1/G2 in a subsequent signed expansion. This minimises new geometry choices and avoids simultaneously varying carrier, projection model and radial law. The PROSE endpoint limit and all Variant-B cells remain separate, potentially later studies. If mathematical exactness cannot be certified, V5B may instead be a signed *bounded numerical approximation* with appropriately weaker reported outcomes, never an exact-class PASS.

No claimed advantage of reciprocal geometry is established by increased projection diversity alone. Even a provably unusual direction-class partition would not imply letter-generation success or specificity without a separately chosen target/noise baseline and fair null calibration.
