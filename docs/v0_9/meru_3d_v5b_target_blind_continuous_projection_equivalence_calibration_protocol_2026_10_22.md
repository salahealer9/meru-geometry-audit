## 5. Mandatory continuous mathematical certificate contract

### 5.1 Mathematically justified quotient, not a sampled similarity rule

For camera `a≠0` and arbitrary 3D differences `U,V`:

`B_a(U,V)=U·V−(a·U)(a·V)/(a·a)`, `D_a(U,V)=a·(U×V)/sqrt(a·a)`.

Require exact generic proof and independent regression of `B_a(U,U)B_a(V,V)−B_a(U,V)^2 = D_a(U,V)^2`, `B_{−a}=B_a`, `D_{−a}=−D_a`, and the proper-frame antipodal reflection. Gram quantities cannot encode the sign of oriented area. No antipodal SO2 pair is merged or separated without its applicable **proof**. In O2 the antipodal reflection identity supplies a full-curve equality proof, not a sampled heuristic.

For each nonconstant projected curve `Y(t)`, define speed `v(t)=sqrt(B_a(X'(t),X'(t)))≥0`, projected length `ℓ=∫₀ᴸ v(t)dt` (not divided by `L`), and `s(t)=∫₀ᵗ v(z)dz`. Before using `s^{-1}`, certify `ℓ>0` and strict increase of `s(t)` on `[0,L]` by analysis/verified enclosure; an isolated zero of `v` is allowable if strict increase is proved. Then `g(s)=Y(t(s))` is a continuous arclength representative, `0≤s≤ℓ`. Unknown inversion is `UNRESOLVED`, not a substituted raw parameter.

### 5.2 Certificate priority, stopping and allowed outcomes

For every camera pair and branch, attempt methods in this deterministic order, sharing an established certificate when the exact logical implication is valid:

1. **Exact generic identities:** reflexivity/consistent frame; antipodal `O2` equality; any exact, prospectively specified rigid group symmetry with a complete proof. Do not assert a new model-specific symmetry from a plotted/sample pattern.
2. **Cheap invariants for UNEQUAL:** rigorous disjoint total projected-length enclosures `ℓ` (allowed because *no geometric scale fitting*); then disjoint squared endpoint-chord enclosures `C=B_a(X(L)−X(0),X(L)−X(0))`. These are necessary under either traversal branch and SO2/O2. Equality/overlap of scalar invariants proves **nothing**.
3. **Oriented witnesses:** for SO2/NO_REV, a nonzero signed determinant of two anchored projected displacements is a sound inequivalence certificate for **antipodal views only**, where exact common-source-point correspondence is already proved; no universal raw-parameter cross-camera rank test is permitted. For SO2/REV (and, if useful, SO2/NO_REV), compute the reversal-invariant `W` below using **certified arclength quartiles**, not index quartiles. Rigorously disjoint W enclosures give UNEQUAL. `W=0`, overlapping enclosures, or failed inversion are inconclusive. `W` may not be used as an O2 orientation test. For general O2, only reflection-even invariants (including `W²` with proved treatment) could serve, and no W-based O2 rule is authorized in this pilot.
4. **Whole-curve kernel proof:** if unresolved, test full necessary and sufficient kernel identities over all real `s,t` in the common length interval. Either supply a symbolic/analytic identity (EQUAL), a globally valid interval or analytic witness of mismatch (UNEQUAL), or leave `UNRESOLVED_PAIR`. A finite list or dense but finite sampling that appears close **cannot** certify EQUAL. No tolerance-based equality classification or numerical optimization over rolls is permitted.

Define anchored arclength displacement `z_g(s)=g(s)−g(0)` and whole kernels `G_g(s,t)=z_g(s)·z_g(t)` and `A_g(s,t)=det(z_g(s),z_g(t))`. After independently proving identical **exact** lengths, equality of entire `G` is necessary and sufficient for translation+O2, and equality of entire `(G,A)` for translation+SO2; rank≤1 can be handled by a separate rigid-extension proof. For reversal use `g_rev(s)=g(ℓ−s)` and reanchor at `g(ℓ)`. Approximate equality, interval overlap, or a finite sample grid is never a whole-curve identity. If exact length equality is unproven, full-kernel equality is not certified.

The orientation witness is:

`o=g(0), e=g(ℓ), m=g(ℓ/2), p1=g(ℓ/4), p3=g(3ℓ/4)`,

`W(g)=det(m−o,e−o) * (||p1−o||²−||p3−e||²)`.

Exact arclength quartiles ensure `W(Rg)=W(g)` under reversal `Rg(s)=g(ℓ−s)`, and `W(Fg)=−W(g)` under reflection. Thus a *certified nonzero* `W(g)` proves inequivalence of `g` and its antipodal mirror even if REV is permitted. `W=0` never proves equality. A numeric `W` merely nonzero in floating point is **not** a certificate. The implementation must explicitly certify quarter-point inverse locations and outward-bound each operation. Avoid treating raw `θ` quartiles or uniform sample indices as arclength quartiles.

### 5.3 No-reversal antipodal rank lemma and caveat
