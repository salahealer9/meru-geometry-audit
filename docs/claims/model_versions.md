# Provisional Model Versions

These labels prevent historically distinct Meru constructions from being
combined prematurely.

## M0 — Planar reciprocal spiral

A two-dimensional baseline based on

\[
r(\theta)=\frac{a}{\theta}.
\]

No torus, tetrahedron, ribbon width, or alphabet comparison is included.

## M1 — Conventional torus embedding

A reciprocal or related spiral mapped onto a standard torus with major radius
\(R\) and minor radius \(r\).

This is a mathematical control model and must not automatically be attributed
to Tenen.

## M2 — Dimpled spherical-torus model

A reconstruction of the apple-like or dimpled spherical surface shown in Meru
materials.

Several candidate surface equations may need to be compared.

## M3 — Finite-width ribbon or flame

A curve thickened into a ribbon, strip, tube, or flame-like surface.

The cross-section and twist law must be explicitly recorded.

## M4 — First Hand model

A reconstruction of the later hand-like object and its claimed letter
silhouettes or gestures.

## M5 — \((3,10)\) torus-knot and tetrahelix model

A separate reconstruction of the reported torus-knot, tetrahedral, and
tetrahelical construction.

## Versioning rule

A model may be labelled as a faithful reconstruction only when the relevant
source provides enough information to determine its geometry.

Otherwise it must be labelled as one of:

- baseline;
- inferred reconstruction;
- candidate reconstruction;
- sensitivity variant;
- null model.
