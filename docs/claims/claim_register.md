## Status vocabulary

- **Unverified** — claim has been located but not independently reproduced.
- **Partially specified** — some mathematical details are explicit, but essential parameters remain missing.
- **Underspecified** — the source does not currently define a unique reconstruction.
- **Reproduced** — the stated construction has been independently implemented.
- **Supported** — the construction survives its preregistered empirical or statistical test.
- **Not supported** — the preregistered test does not support the claim.
- **Historically uncertain** — the source, date, wording, or model version remains unresolved.

## Evidence rule

A visually suggestive resemblance is not by itself considered verification.
Projection claims require explicit geometry, constrained viewing orientations,
objective shape comparisons, and matched controls.
