## 2. Main historical result: the model developed in stages

The Tier-B sources support a multi-stage chronology.

### Stage 1 — abstract vortex/scaffold

Early work produced an abstract vortex/toroidal form before its later identification as a human hand.

### Stage 2 — hand identity recognized

Meru's later historical notes place recognition of the special vortex as a human-hand model around 1991-1992.

The 2006 eTORUS correction says the discovery was so new in 1991 that even the correct handedness had not yet been understood.

### Stage 3 — mathematical refinement after the hand insight

A 1997 interview article hosted by Meru says the tefillin realization disclosed the intended hand identity, but that it then took years to mathematically perfect the shape.

The same article says the finished model incorporates fourteen explicit features.

Those fourteen features are not enumerated in the article or elsewhere in the bounded Tier-B search.

### Stage 4 — algebraic-route investigation

Meru's 2007 historical note quotes the introduction to *The Arm of God*: Tenen says he initially assumed no algebraic function would be involved and expected only a geometric or craft operational definition, then later concluded that algebraic knowledge could have existed in the ancient world.

By the 1996/97 Golden Mean material, Meru explicitly claimed three algebraic function sets and at least one geometric construction.

Thus the algebraic-route claim belongs to a later refinement stage rather than the earliest vortex model.

## 3. Generated form versus arbitrary bending

The 1999 Mishlove interview provides an important interpretive distinction.

Tenen says that one could bend a coat hanger into arbitrary squiggles and obtain letter-like shadows, but that such a result would be meaningless.

He contrasts that with the form he claims is generated by pairing letters in the Hebrew text of Genesis and later identifies the reciprocal equation

\[
r\theta=1.
\]

This historical explanation shows that Meru did not regard arbitrary shadow-matching deformation as sufficient evidence for the model.

That must be kept distinct from the later Golden Mean statement that, for visual and aesthetic purposes, a hand-made vortex can be bent until its ends are parallel and can appear indistinguishable from a generated form.

The source record therefore contains two separate claims:

1. the research legitimacy of the form is supposed to come from a meaningful geometric/textual derivation;
2. a physical display sculpture may be manually adjusted for the desired endpoint appearance.

Tier B does not recover the missing deterministic rule that joins those two claims.

## 4. The fourteen-feature claim

The 1997 Meru-hosted interview article states that the mathematically perfected hand model incorporates fourteen explicit features representing aspects of Western philosophies.

This is a new high-value historical specification claim.

However:

- the fourteen features are not listed;
- their geometric meanings are not given;
- no equations are attached to them;
- no rule is given for combining them into one unique curve.

This strengthens evidence that the final physical/model shape was more constrained than a bare reciprocal spiral.

It does not make the construction reproducible.

## 5. Version-history result: wrong hand and evolving details

The 2006 newsletter records that 1991 footage shows the model worn on the wrong hand because the human-hand interpretation was still new and an important detail had not yet been resolved.

This is direct authorial evidence that:

- the model was still changing after the hand insight;
- early visual sources cannot all be treated as one frozen canonical geometry;
- some discrepancies are version differences rather than contradictions.

This reinforces the audit's version-aware treatment of the 1986-1990s figures.

## 7. Public/private research chronology

Meru's 2007 New Releases page says:

- much of the website/DVD corpus reflects a first mature plateau around 1999;
- the project then continued for years more quietly and privately;
- some materials released later had been held in the background for a decade or more.

This provides a plausible historical explanation for why later pages can refer to refined constructions or features that are absent from the earlier public record.

It does **not** establish what those private materials contained.

It must not be used to assume that the missing algebraic equations existed in a recoverable written form.

## 8. Archive policy and source versioning

Meru's research-archive page explicitly describes its purpose as preserving early hypotheses, including errors and intermediate ideas, to show how the work developed.

Tier B therefore confirms an important methodological rule for this audit:

> early Meru figures and texts are versioned historical evidence, not interchangeable snapshots of one immutable construction.

This supports the existing treatment of:

- turn-count changes;
- wrong-hand corrections;
- refined vortex geometry;
- early hand-drawn projection charts.
