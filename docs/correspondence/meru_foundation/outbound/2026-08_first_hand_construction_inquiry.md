**Subject: Question regarding Stan Tenen’s FIRST HAND geometric construction**

Dear Meru Foundation,

My name is Salah-Eddin Gherbi. I am an independent researcher in the UK, and I have been carrying out a source-led mathematical audit of Stan Tenen’s geometric work, particularly the FIRST HAND model, the reciprocal spiral, the 3,10 structure, and the claimed Hebrew-letter projections.

The purpose of the project is not to dismiss the work, but to determine as carefully as possible which parts of the construction can be reproduced mathematically from the surviving published sources, and which parts remain unspecified.

Over the course of the audit I have been able to recover and reproduce a substantial part of the published geometric framework, including:

* the reciprocal/hyperbolic spiral rθ = 1;
* the later 1.5-turn / 3π FIRST HAND interval;
* the cube-octahedral spherical projection described in The Arm of God;
* the threefold 120° arrangement;
* the 3,10 torus-knot and tetrahedral/tetrahelical framework;
* the six-HAND and seven-region structure;
* and the craftsman’s circle-and-tangent construction described in Meru’s Squaring the Circle material.

There is, however, one important piece that I have not been able to recover from the publicly available material.

In Meru’s discussion of the Golden Mean and logarithmic spirals, Stan states that there are “three sets of specially arranged algebraic functions” and at least one geometric construction that produce apparently identical FIRST HAND forms, with the outer end and inner tip parallel.

I would be very interested to know whether the three algebraic constructions still survive in Meru’s archives.

In particular, I am trying to establish whether any source contains:

* the explicit three sets of algebraic functions;
* their variables, constants, domains, and parameter values;
* the transformation from the reciprocal spiral into the finished three-dimensional FIRST HAND;
* a mathematical definition of the Dimpled-Sphere carrier;
* a quantitative definition of what Stan called the “optimum” hole;
* a derivation showing how the outer end and inner tip become parallel;
* or the list of the “fourteen explicit features” that an older Meru interview says were incorporated into the mathematically perfected hand model.

I am also interested in whether any of the following surviving materials contain further construction details:

* The Dynamics of a Circle and a Line;
* the relevant full issues of TORUS, particularly Vol. 2;
* TetraFlame Graphics;
* The Alphabet in Genesis: From Jerusalem to Sharon — A Record of Meru Foundation Research (1986–2006);
* unpublished notes, diagrams, computer files, or manuscripts by Stan concerning the algebraic definition of FIRST HAND.

If any such material survives, even if it was never formally published, I would be extremely grateful simply to know what exists. If material could be shared, I would preserve its provenance carefully and distinguish clearly between previously published sources, historical unpublished material, and any later reconstruction or explanation.

The current phase of my audit has deliberately stopped rather than filling the missing step with assumptions. Its conclusion is therefore quite narrow: the publicly recoverable material specifies a substantial mathematical and topological scaffold, but I have not yet found a deterministic continuous construction that uniquely produces the finished endpoint-parallel FIRST HAND.

If Meru possesses the missing equations or construction notes, I would very much prefer to study the original material rather than attempt to infer what Stan intended.

Thank you for your time, and also for maintaining so much of the historical Meru material online. It has made it possible to examine the work far more carefully than would otherwise have been possible.
