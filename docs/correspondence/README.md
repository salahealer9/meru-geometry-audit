# Research correspondence

This directory records correspondence relevant to the source provenance
of the Meru Geometry Audit.

Correspondence is preserved to document the origin, date, authorship,
and evidential status of source material that becomes available after
the frozen public-corpus audit.

Material received after a versioned audit checkpoint does not
retroactively alter that checkpoint. It is treated as new evidence and
evaluated in a separately documented addendum or later phase.

Private contact details, personal information, and material not
authorized for redistribution are not committed to the public
repository.
