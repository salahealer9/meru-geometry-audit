# P0A3C — Next Restricted Mathematical Certification Path (Non-Authorizing)

**Prepared:** 2026-10-19. **Status:** preliminary recommendation only. **Not** a signed scientific/engineering protocol, code freeze, test catalogue, numerical certification, selection of final Hebrew representation, or authority to execute. Read alongside the signed P0A3C protocol, the cumulative original ZIP, the independent QC report, and the signed P0A3 preservation plan.

## A. Scientific direction: prove one precise capability first

Repeated attempts to complete the combined QO/QH × A/B engineering domain remained PARTIAL. The next step should **not** repeat that broad request or merely increase the DEV test count. First close one small, mathematically separable domain under new signed authority, publish explicit counterexamples/unsupported conditions, and then expand cautiously. A narrower certified capability is meaningful **as a generic numerical method**, not evidence of Meru/Hebrew specificity.

**Recommended mathematical focus: exact algebraic QO for a single pair of independently supplied, genuinely ordered, open polygonal paths under SO(2).** This is the portion with an analytic planar-roll optimum. Do not choose QO as the primary historical Hebrew score; order of the target's strokes is not yet independently established. The first proof objective should isolate input domain, exact normalizer, branch finiteness and witness validity *before* introducing A/B camera lifts. A physically valid historical shadow law is outside this aim.

## C. Numerical and logical hard cases to preregister before validation

The next signed stage must specify: canonical piecewise-linear arclength interpolation independent of vertex density; repeated consecutive vertices versus genuine retracing; positive extent even on extremely small rational segments; exact comparisons of nested radicals; α=β=0 and both signs of correlations; nearly tied distinct branches; equality only with a proof, not overlapping floating intervals; total/path-specific projection collapse; proper rotations versus reflections; open traversal reversal only when separately authorized; invalid/malformed/nonfinite input; arbitrary bit-growth/resource ceilings; externally anchored source hashes, complete branch/witness checking and tampering-rejection. DEV generic microtests can assist **engineering only** under whatever new signed protocol authorizes them. Registered certificate cases must be separately defined and frozen before their scientific execution.

Mathematically a rational-coordinate polygonal path may have irrational Euclidean segment lengths; therefore an allegedly general *rational-input* method must not silently assume rational arclength. If the next protocol intentionally limits segment lengths to rational values, say so in its scientific estimand, test exclusions, and descriptive name; this smaller domain is different from all rational-coordinate paths. A useful first result can be a proven restricted theorem without relabeling it full-domain certification.

The optima and geometric paths must stay **representation-conditional**. Equal finite support does not imply equal stroke graph or traversals; a zero QO error for separately supplied ordered paths cannot establish a historical 3D construction. A closed-path or multi-stroke extension is a *new* model, not a suppressed nuisance freedom. The 22 basic Hebrew letters remain fixed and not independent statistical replicates.

## D. Decision branch if full algebraic QO is too expensive

If exact real-algebraic comparisons or proof of terminating refinement on general rational inputs prove infeasible, prefer a **separately signed genuinely restricted domain** (for example algebraically simpler path classes with explicitly rational segment arclength and exact finite normalization) with a fully characterized boundary. Then sign a distinct validation protocol for that restricted domain. Preserve negative cases; do not call the restricted guarantee full QO_A/QO_B, all-camera completeness, a primary glyph observable or P0A4-ready implementation under the old scope.

Conversely, if M1–M3 succeed with exact witnesses and a trusted checker, register a small independent **target-free certification** that answers a bounded, reproducible mathematical question. Only after passing that may we attempt all-camera propagation and later candidate/null science.

## E. Unchanged mandatory barriers

No P0A0 S00–S12 replay, Hebrew pixels or fonts as proxies, proxy target shape tuning, candidate/null model run, unregistered optimizer benchmark, SEP/COV sweep, selection of experimental view hierarchy, or P0A4 execution is authorized by this plan. The source-attested reciprocal/1.5-turn/120° constraints do **not** specify the missing three algebraic functions, unique final FIRST HAND, carrier, historical physical shadow or glyph observations. B07 and B08 remain separate mandatory later gates, including rights, independent observations, measured fairness, N0–N3 distributions, target-free detection sensitivity and fixed-corpus inference.

**Recommended next action only after this preservation freeze is signed:** prepare a new narrow mathematical design/preregistration for the single 2D open-path exact QO comparison, with an explicit path to its first independent certificate. Review and sign before any scientific execution or new implementation round. The ultimate general experiment stays conditional on subsequent observation and control gates.
