## 2. Independent mathematical proof review — POSITIVE within the registered restricted theorem domain

**Exact theorem domain**: any finite pair of valid independently ordered **open 2D rational-coordinate polygonal paths** with positive arclength, fixed endpoints/traversals, genuine planar-arclength `du` correspondence, independent center/RMS normalization and **SO(2) rotations only**. **2.1 Positive square-root tower arithmetic — supports theorem.** Every segment length is `sqrt(rational positive)`, knots are ratios of finite algebraic sums, and all moments/correlations are polynomial integrals with algebraic coefficients. A finite tower of *positive* square roots therefore represents the needed quantities. Reducible/dependent adjunctions **must not** be treated as a linearly independent basis. The submitted proof correctly avoids that assumption. For an embedded `x=A+B sqrt(r)` (`r>0` in an earlier tower), exact sign is determined by lower-height signs of `A,B` and, if opposite, `A²−B²r`. The result is `sign(A)*sign(A²−B²r)`; equality is handled when this latter exact sign is zero. Every recursion descends to rational arithmetic, so it terminates in the unrestricted exact-arithmetic model. Test identity `sqrt(3+2sqrt(2))−1−sqrt(2)=0` is indeed a dependent-radical zero, not an approximate coincidence.

**2.2 Inversion in a reducible formal tower — supports theorem.** For `x≠0`, ordinary conjugate inversion is legitimate if `D=A²−B²r≠0`. If `D=0` but `x≠0`, real embedding forces `B sqrt(r)=A` and hence `x=2A` with `A≠0`; inverse reduces to `1/(2A)` in the lower tower. This avoids a false division-by-zero in dependent towers and is algorithmically finite.

**2.3 Normalized arclength, exact integrals and positivity — supports theorem.** Consecutive zero-length edges are bypassable with provenance while genuine retracing retains positive traveled length; nonzero segment durations `l_i/L` are strictly positive. On merged exact algebraic knots, both paths are affine in normalized arclength and their products are quadratic. Exact polynomial antiderivatives (and independently endpoint quadrature identities) give algebraic centroids, RMS variance, `alpha`, and `beta`. For a nonconstant continuous path, positive arclength implies strictly positive variance by continuity of the nonnegative centered integrand, justifying a positive algebraic square root without a numerical floor. Honest subdivision keeps the normalized continuous map; arbitrary resampling does not.

**2.4 Attainment and exact ties — supports theorem.** Put `s=sqrt(alpha²+beta²)`. Every `Q=cI+tJ`, `c²+t²=1`, gives squared residual `2−2(c alpha+t beta)`. Cauchy–Schwarz supplies `s<=1`. For `s=0`, exact zero testing implies `alpha=beta=0`, every proper rotation minimizes, and identity is an attained canonical witness with `q=sqrt(2)`.
