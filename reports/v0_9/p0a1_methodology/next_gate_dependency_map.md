## Fixed corpus and future inference

[METHODOLOGICAL PROPOSAL] The 22 basic letters are a fixed corpus, not 22 IID samples from an alphabet population. Features within a chart and projections from one candidate may be dependent. A 27-form convention is a separate estimand/sensitivity, not five optional replicates. Do not derive precision by treating the corpus as exchangeable independent observations.

Later inferential randomness must be specified through sampled null objects and, separately, any registered extraction-uncertainty mechanism. Freeze the fixed-corpus aggregate and retain per-target errors for coherence/failure accounting, not opportunistic selection. If multiple source-constrained candidates are examined, preregister whether model choice is fixed, integrated or multiplicity-adjusted; choosing the best post-target candidate adds capacity.

For each null family and effective-view tier, freeze effect scale, candidate-versus-null minimum comparison, candidate×capacity interaction, coupling/pairing, sample sizes, Monte Carlo uncertainty/resolution, multiplicity family, planned sensitivity and negative-outcome interpretation. A separately authorized design/power analysis must establish minimum detectable advantage under its assumptions; P0A0 and a corpus count of 22 cannot establish detectability. No statistical test, p-value, power calculation or simulation occurred in P0A1. A future nondetection at registered sensitivity would not prove equality, historical authenticity, alphabet origins or theology.
