## Overview

Version `v0.7.0` completes the native digital-geometry phase of the
Meru 3,10 audit.

The work began with a hand-derived reconstruction of the A10_P03
drawing. That drawing produced a 31-event visible crossing sequence
that failed the classical Gauss even condition. Subsequent source
recovery established why: A10_P03 is a simplified schematic rather
than a complete planar knot projection.

The audit then recovered and independently analysed Meru's official
native `10_3.wrl` model.

## A10_P03 source correspondence

A10_P03 has a strong structural correspondence with `tumble.gif` frame
`000` after an exact vertical reflection:

```text
x' = x
y' = H - 1 - y
```

The correspondence preserves the broad RGB routing, central braid,
outer sweeps and upper/lower loop families.

A10_P03 is therefore treated as a simplified visual schematic. It is
not used independently to determine a complete Gauss word, exact
crossing census, hidden routes or metric embedding.
